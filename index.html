Our Story

In Memory of Stacey Monts

The Stacey Monts Foundation was founded following the sudden and tragic death of Stacey Monts, a 17-year-old who lost his life on July 14, 2018, due to the senseless violence plaguing today's society.

Stacey was a recent high school graduate preparing to go out into the world to begin a new and productive chapter in life. Unfortunately, his life ended far too soon.

The Foundation is determined to remember Stacey by creating a legacy — assisting our youth with basic needs in life, providing a helping hand to families, and creating lasting solutions to improve their security, comfort, and welfare.

Founded By

Shantel Dean, Shante Bradford, Jasmine Benjamin, Amari Kelsey & Ericka Monts

Our Programs

We focus on initiatives that create real, lasting change in our community

Back to School

Equipping students for success with school supplies, backpacks, and educational resources. We believe every child deserves to start the school year prepared and confident.

Thanksgiving Blessing

Providing complete Thanksgiving meals and warm coats to families in need. No family should go hungry during the holidays.

Holiday Joy

Bringing smiles to children's faces with toys, gifts, and holiday cheer. Every child deserves the magic of the holiday season.
